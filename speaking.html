Speaking & media

Mathematics connects a horse’s stride, a swimming jellyfish, and a pumping heart. These talks and stories show how our group studies those connections and brings them into the classroom.

Short biography

Laura A. Miller is a Professor of Mathematics at the University of Arizona and the founder of EquiPhysics. Her research combines mathematical modeling, computation, and experimental measurement to study animal movement and biological fluid dynamics. Her equine work examines how gait, breathing, and heart activity interact, including persistent gait asymmetries in off-the-track Thoroughbreds. The program began at the university’s Al-Marah Equine Center in 2023 and moved with the horses to Rancho del Nido in Sonoita, Arizona, in 2026. Through EquiPhysics, she shares free classroom lessons, apps, and datasets built around real horse movement and physiology. She also develops open-source scientific software, including Fluidlets and pyIBM.
